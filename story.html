Eight Years in the Making

Where the obsession started.

Not every food truck has a story worth telling. Ours started in a kitchen, between a brother and sister who just couldn't stop trying to make things taste better.

Chapter One
01. The Kitchen Years

Charity and Jacob grew up doing what a lot of siblings do — arguing, laughing, and spending hours together in the kitchen. But for them, the kitchen wasn't just somewhere food got made. It was where they watched endless food videos, broke down exactly what made a dish work, and then stayed up way too late trying to recreate it from scratch.

Not satisfied with "pretty good." Never stopping at "close enough." Always back for one more tweak, one more test run, one more family gathering to put it in front of real people and hear the truth — whether they wanted to or not.

"They never stopped until it was right. And even then, Charity was probably already thinking about how to make it better."
02. The Truck

When they decided to start a food truck, they didn't do it halfway. They drove across the country to get a custom truck built exactly to their specs — not off-the-shelf, not close enough, exactly right. They brought it back to Montana and gathered close friends and family to build out the custom cabinets, the details, and all the finishing touches that make a space feel like yours.

Then came the big test run. They invited everyone, cooked everything, and asked for honest feedback. They got it. Back to the trailer. Tweaked, adjusted, improved. That back-and-forth — that refusal to settle — is still how everything works today.

03. Great Falls

For two years, For the Love of Food became the talk of Great Falls, Montana. Events, markets, traveling across the state — people weren't just enjoying the food, they were coming back for it. Asking for more. Telling their friends. The truck that started as a passion project became something the community genuinely counted on.

And then, like life tends to do, things changed. Charity met a studly Canadian and got married. Had a baby. Jacob got married too. The truck went quiet for a while.

"They built something real. Then life happened. And then — better than ever — they came back."
04. The Return

Last year, Charity and Jacob came back together for the first time — this time with their spouses, and with years of living and growing under their belts. They landed in Seeley Lake with the same relentless passion for food, the same refusal to do it halfway, and a whole crew of people who believe in what they're building.

Everything they do is with intention. The ingredients, the recipes, the way they show up for the community — none of it is accidental. It never has been. That's what you taste when you eat their food.

And that's why it's called what it's called.
