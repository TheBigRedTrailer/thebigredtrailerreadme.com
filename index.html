The Short Version

You know the one — The Big Red Trailer.

Two siblings, a custom truck built exactly to their specs, and a relentless obsession with getting the food right. That's the short version of how For the Love of Food became the thing people in Great Falls couldn't stop talking about.

They drove across the country to get the trailer built, threw a huge gathering to test everything, took every piece of feedback — good and painfully honest — went back, tweaked, improved, and came out swinging. Montana noticed.

Then life happened. Now they're back, better than ever, and The Big Red Trailer is rolling again.
